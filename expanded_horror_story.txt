In the year 2021, a reclusive and obscenely wealthy tech billionaire made a decision that would remain hidden from the public for years to come. 

Under the cover of absolute secrecy, he commissioned the construction of a private vault deep beneath the rugged terrain of New Zealand. Nestled far below layers of bedrock, concrete, and reinforced steel, the facility was marketed on paper as nothing more than an apocalyptic survival bunker. The world was told it was a precautionary measure - a place to ride out the worst the world could possibly throw at humanity.

But the truth, as it always does, began to slip through the cracks.

Last week, a heavily redacted blueprint was leaked to an independent investigative journal. Buried within the schematics, hidden behind layers of technical jargon that only a structural engineer would think to question, was something utterly horrifying.

The vault's locking mechanisms had not been designed the way any sane bunker would be. They were not built to seal people inside for their own protection in the event of catastrophe. 

They were built to lock from the outside.

With chilling mathematical precision, every one of the massive, hydraulic locks was calculated to engage and seal tight - not to keep threats out, but to keep something massive locked in. Something that, whatever it was, had to never see the light of day again.

The leak caused immediate panic amongst those who managed to get their eyes on it. But it wasn't until yesterday that the situation went from deeply unnerving to genuinely apocalyptic in scope.

Satellite thermal imaging, obtained through a backchannel from a climate research satellite, picked up activity from the coordinates of the long abandoned construction site. Despite the vault having been declared "empty" and sealed years ago with no staff or power supposed to be running within it, the internal temperature had spiked dramatically.

In the span of mere hours, thermometers read off the charts. The temperature inside the massive subterranean complex had risen to over one thousand degrees Celsius.

There were no generators. No working power grid. No known heat sources.

Something down there was waking up. And whatever it was, it was getting very, very hot with rage.

For now, the world doesn't know its name. But for those who have seen the blueprint and the thermal readings, one question haunts them above all else:

Whatever they locked down there all those years ago... is it trying to get out?
