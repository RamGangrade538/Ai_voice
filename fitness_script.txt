Ever wonder why some guys pack on muscle while others grind for years and see nothing? 

It's not about how sore you get, or how big your pump is. It's about what's happening inside your body.

When you lift with control against resistance, your muscles feel tension. That's the big one. Cells sense it, turn on growth signals, and start building more contractile protein. Heavy swings don't cut it - controlled tension does.

You might've heard you need to tear your muscles to grow. Not true. Damage can happen, especially with new moves or slow negatives, but it's not required. Chasing soreness just slows you down.

And that pump? Feels great, looks bigger for a bit, but it's just blood and fluid. Temporary. Growth comes from consistent tension over time, not maxing out the burn.

You can grow with heavier weights for fewer reps, or lighter weights for more reps - as long as it's challenging. What matters is applying tension, recovering well, and progressively doing more over time.

Do that consistently, and the size comes. Simple as that.
