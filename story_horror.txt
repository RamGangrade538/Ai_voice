In 2021, a famous tech billionaire built a private, deep-underground vault in New Zealand, claiming it was for apocalypse survival. But last week, a leaked blueprint revealed something terrifying. The vault locks don't close from the inside to keep people out. They are mathematically designed to lock from the outside, keeping something massive... in. And yesterday, satellite thermal images showed that the temperature inside the empty vault suddenly spiked to over one-thousand degrees.
